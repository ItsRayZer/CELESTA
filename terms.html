TERMS & CONDITIONS.

The rules for taking part in SPARK 2K26.

1. The fest

SPARK 2K26 is an inter-school tech fest organised by NEXUS, the Computer Applications Department Association of Mar Augusthinose College, Ramapuram. It takes place on Friday, 27 November 2026.

2. Registration

Registration online is free. Event fees are paid at the venue on the day of the fest. Every participant must report by 9:30 AM with a valid school ID card.

3. Eligibility

Open to school students only. One team per student, per event. Team sizes are fixed per event and shown on the Events page.

4. Fees

  • Reels Competition — ₹100 per team
  • Prompting — ₹50
  • Quiz — ₹100 per team
  • Treasure Hunt — ₹200 per team

Fees are paid at the venue on 27 November 2026.

5. Prizes

₹14,250 in total prizes across the four events, as listed on the event pages. Treasure Hunt is winner-takes-all: ₹6,000 to the first team that finds the treasure.

6. Conduct

Participants must follow the coordinators’ instructions, maintain discipline, and not cheat, share answers, damage college property, or disturb others. Offensive or inappropriate content is not allowed. The organisers’ decision is final.

7. Reels

The college reserves the right to use, share, publish or reproduce the submitted reels on its official social media platforms, website and other promotional materials.

8. Changes

The organisers may change event details, timings or prizes if needed. Updates will be posted on this website and on Instagram @sparkk.2026.

9. Contact

Questions? Talk to the team on the Contact page.
