POWERED BY MAR AUGUSTHINOSE COLLEGE, RAMAPURAM PRESENTED BY NEXUS · COMPUTER APPLICATIONS DEPARTMENT ASSOCIATION

CELESTA 2K26, THE INTER-COLLEGE TECH FEST OF MAR AUGUSTHINOSE COLLEGE, RAMAPURAM, STANDS AS A SYMBOL OF YOUNG INNOVATION, CREATIVITY, AND EXCELLENCE. PRESENTED BY NEXUS, THE BCA DEPARTMENT ASSOCIATION, CELESTA PROVIDES A DYNAMIC ARENA WHERE COLLEGE STUDENTS, YOUNG INNOVATORS, AND TECHNOPHILES ACROSS KERALA COME TOGETHER TO IDEATE, COMPETE, AND SHOWCASE THEIR TALENTS.
