Celesta is the inter-school tech fest of the BCA department — a single day where students from schools across Kerala gather on one campus to compete, create and celebrate. Five arenas, one stage, and a prize pool of ₹17,250.

Celesta 2K26 is the inter-school tech fest presented by NEXUS, the BCA Department Association of Mar Augusthinose College, Ramapuram — one day, one campus, five arenas.
