PRIVACY POLICY.

What we collect, and what we do with it.

1. What we collect

When you register, we collect your name, school, phone number, team name, and the names of your other team members. Email is optional.

2. Why we collect it

Only to run SPARK 2K26: to register your team and contact you about the fest. Nothing else.

3. What we never do

We never share or sell your details. We run no ads and no analytics of our own. The site does load fonts, maps and libraries from Google and Cloudflare, which receive standard request data (such as your IP address) when your browser fetches them.

4. Your consent

By ticking the checkbox on the registration form, you agree to your details being used only for fest registration.

5. Deletion

Ask the organisers any time to delete your details, and they will be removed. See the Terms & Conditions for how the fest is run.

6. Young participants

SPARK is an inter-school fest, so most participants are school students. Details are collected only for fest registration, with consent.

7. Contact

Questions about your data? Talk to the team on the Contact page.
